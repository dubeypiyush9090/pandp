Investigative Journalism

Investigative reporting sachchai ko surface par laane ka kaam karta hai. Yahan hum hypothesis se lekar publication tak ke stages aur best practices ko explore karenge.

Investigative Process

1. Subject discover karna

Investigation ki shuruat ek potential scandal ya unexplored issue se hoti hai. Government documents, whistleblower hints ya public rumours se idea mil sakta hai【787327611738009†L1006-L1014】.

2. Hypothesis banana

Ek working hypothesis tayaar karein – aapko kis baat ka proof chahiye aur kyun. Yeh hypothesis investigation ko direction deta hai【787327611738009†L1006-L1014】.

3. Open source research

Public records, RTI (Right to Information) requests, court documents, company filings aur data sources ka istemal karke evidence collect karein. Online databases aur archived news articles helpfull hote hain【787327611738009†L1006-L1014】.

4. Human sources

Insiders aur experts se interview karein. Confidentiality agreements aur safety ka dhyan rakhein. Story ke multiple viewpoints lene se credibility badhti hai【787327611738009†L1006-L1014】.

5. Data organise & analyse

Sari collected information ko timeline aur categories mein organise karein. Document management tools aur spreadsheets ka istemal karein. Evidence ko cross‑verify karte hue contradictions aur patterns pe focus karein【787327611738009†L1006-L1014】.

6. Narrative & quality control

Story ko narrative sequence mein arrange karein, jahan facts logically flow karein. Publication se pehle quality control jaise legal vetting, ethical checks aur fairness ke principles follow karein【787327611738009†L1006-L1014】.

7. Publish & promote

Final report publish hone ke baad usse promote karein – TV, digital platforms, podcasts ya print. Social media par audience ko engage karein aur authorities ko accountable hold karne ke liye follow‑up stories plan karein【787327611738009†L1006-L1014】.

Ethics & Safety

Investigative journalists ko hamesha ethical guidelines follow karne chahiye. Fake evidence ya entrapment ka sahara na lein. Sensitive sources ko anonymous rakhna aur whistleblowers ki safety ensure karna zaroori hai. Legal advice aur editor ke consultation ke bina controversial stories publish na karein.
